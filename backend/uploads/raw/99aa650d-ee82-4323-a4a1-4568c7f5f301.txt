Notes Meta ADS Campaign

1. Awareness :- 
Show your ads to people who are most likely remember them.
                 
                Reach,Brand awareness,Video views,Store location awareness
What it optimizes for:
Ad recall
Maximum reach (CPM optimization)
📊 Metrics:
Impressions
Reach
Frequency
CPM (cost per 1000 impressions)
🎯 When to use:
New brand / zero audience
Product launch
Local business visibility
⚠️ Truth:

👉 Awareness ≠ sales
You’re paying for eyeballs, not buyers

💡 Pro Strategy:
Use short videos (5–10 sec)
Hook in first 2 seconds
Broad targeting (no overthinking)

2.Trffic :- 
📌 What it optimizes for:
Link clicks
Landing page views
📊 Metrics:
CPC (cost per click)
CTR (click-through rate)
Landing page views
🎯 When to use:
Affiliate marketing
Blog/content traffic
Portfolio visits
⚠️ Biggest mistake:

👉 Traffic audience = clickers, not buyers

Example:
You get 1000 clicks → 0 sales 😅

💡 Pro Strategy:
Only use for warm-up funnel
Retarget traffic with Sales ads later

3. ENGAGEMENT

📌 What it optimizes for:
Likes, comments, shares
Video views
Messages (DMs)
📊 Metrics:
Engagement rate
Cost per engagement
Video watch %
🎯 When to use:
Build trust & credibility
Make post viral
Start conversations
💡 Real Use Case:

Run engagement ad → post gets 1000+ likes
Then reuse same post for Sales ads (with social proof)

⚠️ Truth:

👉 Engagement doesn’t equal revenue
But it boosts conversion later


4. LEADS (Client Favorite 💼)



📌 What it optimizes for:
Form submissions
Contact info (email, phone)
📊 Metrics:
CPL (cost per lead)
Conversion rate
Lead quality
🎯 When to use:
Freelancers (like YOU 💥)
Coaching, real estate, services
Local businesses
🔧 Types:
Instant forms (inside Facebook) → cheap leads
Website forms → high quality leads
💡 Pro Strategy:
Ask fewer questions → more leads
Add qualifying question → better clients

Example:
“What is your monthly budget?”


5. APP PROMOTION



📌 What it optimizes for:
App installs
In-app actions
🎯 When to use:
SaaS apps
Mobile startups
⚠️ Needs:
Meta SDK installed
Event tracking setup

👉 Not useful for beginners unless you have an app


6. SALES (Conversion King 👑)

📌 What it optimizes for:
Purchases
Add to cart
Checkout
📊 Metrics:
CPA (cost per acquisition)
ROAS (return on ad spend)
Conversion rate
🎯 When to use:
E-commerce
Affiliate marketing (advanced)
Any business wanting revenue
🔥 Requirements:
Meta Pixel installed
Conversion events tracked
💡 Pro Strategy:
Start with Add to Cart optimization
Then move to Purchase optimization
